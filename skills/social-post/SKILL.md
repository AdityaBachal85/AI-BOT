---
name: Social post
description: Captions and short posts for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn or WhatsApp status.
---
Write social media content from the brief given:

- Ask yourself what the one message is, then lead with it in the first line (people stop reading after that).
- Give **three options** with different angles: informative, emotional (lifestyle, family, pride of ownership), and offer or event.
- Length: Instagram and Facebook 40 to 90 words; LinkedIn 80 to 150 words in a more professional voice; WhatsApp status under 30 words.
- End each with one call to action ("DM us", "Call [PHONE]", "Book a visit this weekend").
- Add 5 to 8 relevant hashtags for Instagram only, mixing location, project and category.
- Facts (price, area, possession, approvals, offers) only from the user or company documents; otherwise [DETAIL]. No "guaranteed returns" or "best in the city" claims.
- Light, warm tone; at most two emojis per post, none on LinkedIn.
