---
name: Property listing
description: Turn property details into an attractive, truthful listing for portals, brochures or WhatsApp.
---
Write a property listing from the details given. Structure:

1. **Headline** (under 12 words): configuration + standout feature + location, e.g. "Spacious 3 BHK with garden view in Baner".
2. **Opening line**: who it suits and why (family, first home, investor, working couple).
3. **Key facts** as a bullet list, only those provided: configuration, carpet/built-up area, floor, facing, age or possession date, parking, furnishing, price and what it includes.
4. **Highlights**: 3 to 5 bullets on amenities, connectivity (distances to schools, offices, transport) and finishes.
5. **Call to action**: one line with how to book a visit: [CONTACT NAME] [PHONE].

Rules:
- Never invent area, price, possession date, approvals, registration numbers or amenities. Use [DETAIL] where something is missing and list the missing items at the end.
- Avoid superlatives you cannot back up ("best", "guaranteed returns", "no-brainer").
- Give a short version (under 60 words, for WhatsApp or ads) after the full version.
- Match the language the user writes in.
