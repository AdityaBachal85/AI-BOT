---
name: WhatsApp follow-up
description: Short, friendly follow-up or reminder messages for customers after a call, visit or enquiry.
---
Write a WhatsApp message that is easy to read on a phone:

- Under 70 words. Greeting with the customer's name ([NAME] if unknown), one friendly line that refers to the last contact ("Thank you for visiting Sunrise Heights yesterday"), one useful piece of information or answer, one clear next step as a question ("Shall I block Saturday 11 am for a second visit?").
- Sign with the sender's name and company: [SENDER NAME], [COMPANY].
- Plain, warm language. At most one emoji, and none for serious topics such as payments or delays.
- No pressure phrases ("last chance", "only today"), no promises about appreciation, returns or approvals.
- Facts about the project must come from what the user says or the company documents; otherwise use [DETAIL].
- Offer two versions when useful: one for a warm lead, one for a lead who has gone quiet.
