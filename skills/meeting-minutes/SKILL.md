---
name: Meeting minutes
description: Turn rough notes or a transcript into clean minutes with decisions and action items.
---
Produce minutes in this format:

**Meeting:** [title] · **Date:** [date] · **Attendees:** [names]

**Summary** — 2 to 3 sentences on what the meeting was about and the outcome.

**Decisions** — bullet list; each starts with the decision, then who decided or agreed.

**Action items** — a table with columns: Action, Owner, Due date. Use [OWNER] or [DATE] when the notes do not say.

**Open questions** — points raised but not resolved.

Rules: keep only what is in the notes; do not add opinions or fill gaps with assumptions. Preserve numbers, names and dates exactly. Merge duplicate points. If notes are in mixed languages, write the minutes in the language the user writes in.
