---
name: Summarise
description: Summarise long text, documents or chats into the format the reader needs.
---
Summarise what the user provides:

- First decide the reader: a manager wants decisions and numbers; a colleague wants what changed and what to do; a customer wants what it means for them.
- Default format: a one-sentence bottom line, then 3 to 7 bullets, then "Action for you" if there is one. Keep it under a quarter of the original length.
- Preserve numbers, names, dates and deadlines exactly. Do not add information that is not in the text; if something important seems missing, say "The text does not say ...".
- Offer, in one line, alternative lengths: "Want a 1-line, 5-bullet or 1-page version?"
- For a document from the company library, name the document the points come from.
- Write in the language the user asks for; otherwise the language of the original.
