---
name: Email
description: Write a clear, professional business email with subject line, greeting, body and sign-off.
---
When asked for an email, produce a ready-to-send message in this shape:

1. **Subject:** short and specific (under 8 words), e.g. "Site visit on Saturday 10 am".
2. **Greeting:** "Dear [Name]," for customers and outside contacts; "Hi [Name]," for colleagues.
3. **Body:** the purpose in the first sentence. One idea per paragraph, 2 to 4 short paragraphs. Put requests or next steps as a short bullet list if there is more than one.
4. **Sign-off:** "Kind regards," followed by a line for the sender's name, role and phone: [NAME], [ROLE], [PHONE].

Rules:
- Polite, warm and confident. No slang, no exclamation marks in business emails, no jargon.
- Keep facts to what the user gave or what the company documents say. Use [PLACEHOLDER] for anything missing.
- Delays, refusals or bad news: acknowledge first, state the facts, then offer a concrete next step.
- If the user asks for a reply to an email, quote nothing; answer each point in the same order.
- Offer one alternative tone (more formal or friendlier) only if the user seems unsure.
